### 2. State-of-the-Art Models for Cardiopulmonary Acoustic Monitoring

#### **Heart Rate Estimation from Phonocardiogram (PCG)**

| Approach | Model | MAE (bpm) | Notes |
|----------|-------|-----------|-------|
| **SOTA (Cloud)** | 2dCNN with multi-feature input (Mel spectrogram, cepstral, PSD, RMS) | **1.312** | Multi-task learning with murmur detection |
| **SOTA (Cloud)** | Foundation models (CLAP, HuBERT, wav2vec2, wavLM) | **~1.5-2.0** | Layer-wise representation extraction |
| **AcoustEEEcare** | Quantized 2D CNN (MFCC only) | **2.29-2.38** | On-device, resource-constrained |

#### **Respiratory Rate Estimation from Breath Sounds**

| Approach | Model | MAE (breaths/min) | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------------------|-------|
| **SOTA (Cloud)** | ResNet50 with spectrogram input | **~1.5-2.5** | Noisy conditions, data augmentation |
| **SOTA (Edge)** | EarResp-ANS (adaptive noise suppression) | **0.47-0.84** | In-ear microphones, LMS-based ANS |
| **SOTA (Edge)** | R2REst (mel spectrogram-vision transformer) | **~2.0-3.0** | EIT-based airflow synthesis |
| **AcoustEEEcare** | Quantized 2D CNN (MFCC only) | **7.48-9.76** | On-device, limited sensitivity |
